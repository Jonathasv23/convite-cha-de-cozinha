## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an editorial, intimate, and tactile stationery aesthetic tailored for bridal and kitchen showers (*chá de cozinha / chá de panela*). Balancing high-end bridal luxury with inviting warmth, the visual language draws inspiration from bespoke letterpress stationery, fine botanical linework, and artisanal handmade paper.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Core
- **Graceful & Poetic**: Classical serif typography with generous leading evokes timeless romance and deliberate celebration.
- **Intimate & Welcoming**: Warm cream, sage, and deep botanical olive create an earthy, grounded sanctuary free of sterile tech conventions.
- **Artisanal & Tactile**: Delicate borders, embossed-style card containers, and quiet champagne accents echo luxury paper goods and hot-stamped gold foil.

### Design Movement
An elevated fusion of **Editorial Fine-Art Stationery** and **Contemporary Warm Minimalism**. Content flows with generous breathing room, relying on nuanced tonal layering and subtle ambient warmth rather than aggressive contrast.

## Colors

The palette grounds the experience in nature and refined celebratory warmth.

- **Primary (`#3D4A36` - Heritage Olive)**: Deep, grounded botanical green. Serves as the dominant anchor for critical calls-to-action (RSVP buttons), primary titles, and formal headers.
- **Secondary (`#8A9A80` - Warm Sage)**: Soft, leafy intermediate tone. Applied to active states, secondary badges, soft iconography, and supportive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880` - Champagne Gold)**: Understated metallic warmth. Used sparingly for hairline divider flourishes, decorative monograms, confirmation tags, and subtle metallic rim highlights.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F7F2` - Ivory Parchment)**: Warm, tactile off-white evoking natural cotton cardstock. Softens optical strain and provides authentic warmth over cold digital whites.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Vellum)**: Utilized for foreground cards, registry items, and input fields to lift content cleanly over the ivory canvas.
- **Text Hierarchy**:
  - Headings & Primary Text: `#20281D` (Deep Olive Black)
  - Secondary/Body Copy: `#545E50` (Subdued Olive Slate)
  - Muted/Metadata: `#7C8877` (Muted Laurel)
  - Border Accents: `#E4E0D5` (Warm Flax) and `#D1C7B7` (Soft Linen)

## Typography

The pairing reconciles literary elegance with effortless transactional clarity.

- **EB Garamond (Headlines, Display, Monograms, Quotes)**: Classical humanistic proportions, graceful serifs, and historic italics evoke traditional letterpress wedding invitations. Applied across large headings, hero couple titles (*"Chá de Panela de Helena & Gabriel"*), and date callouts.
- **Manrope (Body, Form Labels, Interactive UI, Registry Details)**: Modern, highly balanced geometric grotesque with open apertures. Ensures immediate legibility across gift lists, logistical itineraries, address directions, and RSVP form inputs.
- **Casing & Micro-Details**: `label-md` and `label-lg` tokens utilize gentle uppercase tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) for categorical metadata, dates, and form labels, establishing refined hierarchy against Garamond display elements.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an editorial column architecture with restrained max-widths to mimic luxury physical paper goods.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column grid, max container width `1120px` (or `760px` for focused single-column narratives such as RSVP & Story sections). Gutters at `1.5rem`, outer section margins at `3rem` up to `4rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` side margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Emphasizes intentional pause and vertical rhythm. Key milestone sections (e.g., *Confirmação de Presença*, *Lista de Presentes*, *Como Chegar*) use `space-2xl` boundaries to create a calm, unhurried reading flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows, relying instead on tactile paper layering, warm ambient light, and double-hairline borders.

- **Ambient Warm Shadows**:
  - **Level 1 (Stationery Resting)**: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(61, 74, 54, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(61, 74, 54, 0.03)` — used for cards, gift tiles, and text input fields.
  - **Level 2 (Stationery Lifted / Hover)**: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(61, 74, 54, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(61, 74, 54, 0.04)` — applied to interactive cards on hover, active modal sheets, and primary callout cards.
  - **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs)**: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(32, 40, 29, 0.14)` — reserved for full RSVP confirmations, gift reservation drawers, and toast alerts.
- **Delicate Paper Inset & Borders**: Cards integrate a continuous `1px` outer stroke in `#E4E0D5`. Premium card components feature an internal pseudo-border offset by `4px` or `6px` in `#D1C7B7` or muted gold `#C5A880` at 40% opacity, recreating embossed stationery borders.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Applied to buttons, form inputs, counter badges, and informational chips.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`)**: Applied to content cards, gift list modules, itinerary timeline blocks, and map preview frames.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`)**: Applied to floating invitation panels, hero card wraps, and bottom drawers on mobile.
- **Circle (`9999px`)**: Utilized for decorative floral monogram seals, avatar thumbnails, and icon buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (*"Confirmar Presença"*)**: Solid `#3D4A36` background, `#F8F7F2` typography in `label-lg`, 8px border radius. Padding: `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle transition to `#2D3728` with Level 2 warm shadow on hover.
- **Secondary Action (*"Ver Lista de Presentes"*)**: Transparent background with a `1px` border in `#3D4A36`, text in `#3D4A36`. Hovers into `#F0EFEA`.
- **Tertiary / Link (*"Copiar Chave PIX"*)**: Text in `#3D4A36` with a gentle underline in `#C5A880`, paired with an inline copy icon.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Input & Textarea**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E4E0D5`, border-radius 8px. Text styled in `body-md`. Focus state transitions border to `#3D4A36` with a faint `2px` focus ring in `rgba(138, 154, 128, 0.25)`.
- **Labels**: Rendered in `label-md` uppercase with `#545E50`, accompanied by an asterisk in `#C5A880` for mandatory entries (*"NOME COMPLETO *"*, *"RESTRIÇÕES ALIMENTARES"*).

### Cards & Stationery Enclosures
- **Invitation Hero Card**: Off-white parchment background with a subtle inset hairline border, centered classical serif headings, decorative botanical divider, and couple initials enclosed within an embossed circle.
- **Gift Registry Card (*Lista de Presentes*)**: Pure white container with Level 1 shadow. Shows item photo or refined icon, gift title in Garamond, status tag (*"Disponível"* in `#8A9A80` or *"Presenteado"* in `#C5A880`), and an action button to reserve or contribute via PIX.

### Checkboxes, Radio Buttons & Toggle Chips
- Custom circular indicators. Selected state fills with `#3D4A36` displaying a delicate check icon.
- **Dietary & Attendance Chips**: Pill-shaped or 8px rounded tags with `1px solid #E4E0D5` and `#545E50` text; active state fills with `#EAECE7` and activates a `#3D4A36` border with bold text.

### Timeline & Itinerary List
- Vertical layout connected by a delicate `1px` dashed hairline in `#C5A880`.
- Each milestone (*"16:00 - Recepção & Boas-vindas"*, *"17:30 - Brincadeiras & Chá"*, *"19:00 - Brinde & Doces"*) features an olive node badge and Garamond milestone title.
